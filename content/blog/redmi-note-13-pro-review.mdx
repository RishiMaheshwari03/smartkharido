        The Redmi Note 13 Pro immediately feels more premium than its price suggests. Xiaomi has put Corning Gorilla Glass Victus on both the front and back — something you typically only see on phones costing ₹10,000 more. The curved edges make it comfortable to hold despite its size.

        The frosted glass back resists fingerprints well, and the camera module protrudes only slightly. Available in Midnight Black, Arctic White, and Coral Purple — the Purple variant has become particularly popular in India.

        At 187g it is on the heavier side, and one-handed use on the 6.67 inch screen takes some adjustment. The IP54 rating means it will survive light rain and splashes — important for Indian weather conditions.

        **Build verdict:** Premium look and feel that punches above its price. Gorilla Glass Victus protection is a genuine differentiator in this segment.

        This is where the Redmi Note 13 Pro genuinely excels. The 6.67 inch AMOLED panel with 1220×2712 resolution and 120Hz refresh rate is the best display you can get under ₹20,000 in India — full stop.

        Colours are vivid and accurate. Blacks are true blacks. The 120Hz adaptive refresh rate makes scrolling through apps, Instagram reels, and YouTube feel noticeably smoother than phones with standard 60Hz displays.

        Outdoor visibility is excellent — you can comfortably read the screen under direct sunlight, which matters a lot in India. The 1800 nits peak brightness means HDR content on YouTube and Netflix looks spectacular.

        The always-on display feature is also well-implemented and does not drain battery noticeably.

        **Display verdict:** Class-leading. No other phone at this price point comes close to this display quality.

    - id: camera
      title: Camera Samples & Verdict
      icon: "📷"
      score: 8
      image: "https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1606983340126-99ab4feaa64a?w=700&q=80&auto=format"
      content: |
        The 200MP headline number is real — but the phone defaults to 12.5MP shots using pixel-binning, which produces better results in most lighting conditions than shooting full 200MP.

        In good daylight, the main camera produces genuinely impressive results. Detail, dynamic range, and colour accuracy are all excellent. Portrait mode creates professional-looking bokeh.

        Low light performance is good but not exceptional. Night mode works well for static subjects, but moving subjects in dim light still show some blur — a limitation of the sensor size, not software.

        The ultrawide camera at 8MP is the weak link. Quality drops noticeably compared to the main lens. This is the one area where Samsung M35 has an advantage.

        Front camera at 16MP produces flattering selfies with good skin tone accuracy for Indian skin tones specifically.

        **Camera verdict:** Best-in-class main camera at this price. Disappointing ultrawide. Overall still the strongest camera system under ₹20,000.

        The 5000mAh battery comfortably lasts a full day of heavy Indian usage — WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, Google Maps, and calls combined. With moderate use, 1.5 days is achievable.

        The real standout is the 67W turbo charger included in the box. Zero to 100% in under 50 minutes means a 15-minute charge before leaving home gives you meaningful battery life.

        Screen-on time averages 6.5 to 7 hours in real-world Indian usage conditions, which is excellent for a 120Hz AMOLED display.

        **Battery verdict:** Strong battery life combined with genuinely fast charging makes this one of the least stressful phones to own at this price.

        The Dimensity 7200 Ultra handles everything a typical Indian user throws at it without breaking a sweat. WhatsApp, Chrome with 15 tabs, Instagram, Google Maps running simultaneously — all smooth with no lag.

        For gaming: BGMI runs at High settings comfortably. Call of Duty Mobile handles High graphics. For Ultra or Extreme settings in BGMI, there is occasional frame drop — but this is a mid-range chip, not a gaming chip.

        The 8GB RAM variant is sufficient for most users. The 12GB variant makes sense if you frequently switch between many apps or game seriously.

        Heat management is reasonable. Extended gaming sessions cause the phone to get warm but not uncomfortably hot — better than most competitors in this range.

        HyperOS ships with 8 to 10 pre-installed apps that cannot be uninstalled. This is the most common complaint from Indian buyers and a legitimate annoyance.

        **Performance verdict:** More than capable for daily use and casual gaming. Bloatware is annoying but manageable. Not a gaming phone — buy Poco X6 if gaming is your priority.
