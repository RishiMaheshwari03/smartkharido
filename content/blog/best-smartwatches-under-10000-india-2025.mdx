Finding a good smartwatch under ₹10,000 in India is harder than it looks. The market is flooded with look-alike devices, inflated specs, and brands with zero after-sales support.

We compared 12 models, checked real user reviews, verified service centre availability across India, and shortlisted only the ones we would actually recommend to a friend.

## What to Check Before Buying

**Display: AMOLED vs LCD**
AMOLED is brighter, more vibrant, and power-efficient. At this range, only Noise ColorFit Ultra 3 and Fastrack offer AMOLED. Worth prioritising if you use your watch outdoors in sunlight.

**After-Sales Service in India**
The most underrated factor. Unknown brands may have no service centres. Stick to Noise, boAt, Fastrack, Amazfit, or Fire-Boltt — all have verified service support across India.

**Water Resistance Ratings Explained**

- IP67 — Splash and sweat safe only
- IP68 — Brief submersion safe
- 5ATM — Swimming safe

**Battery Life**
Anything under 5 days real-world becomes inconvenient fast. All 5 picks above clear this bar comfortably.
