## What to Look For When Buying a Laptop Under ₹50,000

**RAM:** Always go for at least 8GB. 4GB will feel slow within a year.

**Storage:** SSD is non-negotiable. A laptop with HDD storage will feel painfully slow.

**Processor:** AMD Ryzen 5 or Intel Core i5 (12th gen or newer) are the sweet spots at this price range.

**Display:** Look for IPS panels — they have better viewing angles and colour accuracy than TN panels.
